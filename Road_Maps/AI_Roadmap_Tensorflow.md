## Learning Rules

1. Understand the theory before using a framework.
2. Understand the mathematics behind important algorithms.
3. Implement important algorithms from scratch.
4. Use established frameworks after understanding the fundamentals.
5. Build a practical project after every major phase.
6. Read research papers when reaching advanced topics.
7. Maintain notes and document your experiments.
8. Never skip model evaluation.
9. Learn how models fail, not just how they work.
10. Focus on production-quality AI engineering.
